# Hi, I'm Keith (Chun Yiu Lau) 👋

I design and build learning products, mainly maths. I like taking a messy learning
problem, working out why students get stuck, and turning that into something they can
play with.

- 🧮 **Math AI**: adaptive maths platform for Hong Kong schools, co-developed with
  The Education University of Hong Kong. 30+ paying schools, 2,000+ users and
  150,000+ questions completed in 18 months. I was product owner and lead engineer.
- 🏘️ **Math Town**: a game layer over Math AI. Students explore a town, earn rewards for
  practice, and collect trophies. Story missions are in design.
- 🌍 **Maths modelling lessons** (in progress): everyday situations students play
  through to discover which maths sits behind them.
- 🔬 **Stematics**: my STEAM education company. Science learned through
  hands-on experiments and everyday life.

## How I work
1. Break the curriculum into learning points and prerequisite links.
2. Design a sequence: concept → basic operation → multi-step → applied problem.
3. Build interactive visuals so students can see the idea, not just compute it.
4. Use students' errors to find the gap that sits underneath.
